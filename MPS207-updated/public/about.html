Owner & Master Plumber

Keith McGillivary

Keith started as a helper for a small plumbing outfit, then went through Southern Maine Community College's plumbing and heating program while still working under his mentor, Russell. He stayed on with Russell for three more years after graduating — then spent six years as a pipefitter at Bath Iron Works, testing piping systems on naval destroyers.

Nights and weekends during that stretch went toward logging the hours for his Master Plumber license. Once he had it, he went fully self-employed — not to maximize revenue, but for the flexibility to actually be around for his family.

“Everyone expects a doctor to make six figures, but not everyone knows you can make that in the trades without massive student loan debt.”

What He's About

Family First, Trade Second — But Never Cutting Corners on Either

Keith sets firm boundaries on his hours and takes real time off, because the point of running MPS was never to work himself into the ground — it was to build something that gives his family time back. Off the clock, he's usually camping in the summer or snowmobiling in the winter (his goal: the Great Gaspe Snowmobile Tour). On the job, that same "do it right, keep it honest" approach is exactly what 30,000+ people tune in to watch on Instagram.
